Privacy Policy for Flap Bird

Application: Flap Bird | Developer: TG Games | Effective Date: September 29, 2026
Summary: Flap Bird is a free-to-play arcade game. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect personal identifiers such as your real name, physical address, or phone number. Third-party ad partners (Google AdMob) may collect standard advertising identifiers to deliver sponsored rewards (such as revives and in-game coins).

1. Information We Collect

Flap Bird operates primarily on your device:

  • Local Game Data: Your high scores, accumulated coins, unlocked skins, sound settings, and world choices are stored locally on your device storage. This data is not uploaded to any private server.
  • Device and Advertising Information: When you connect to the internet, third-party advertising SDKs automatically receive standard technical information, including:
    • Google Advertising ID (GAID)
    • Device manufacturer, model, and OS version
    • IP address and general coarse geographical location (country/city level)
    • Ad interaction and performance metrics (e.g. video completion for rewarded revives)

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Flap Bird uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device for ad delivery and analytics:

  • Google Play Services: Used for core Android framework integration.
    Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Used to display interstitial and rewarded video ads that grant second-chance revives and in-game currency.
    How Google uses information from sites or apps

3. How We Use Collected Information

The information collected through third-party services is used exclusively to:

  • Deliver gameplay mechanics (rewarded ads for extra lives and free coins).
  • Prevent ad fraud and ensure ad network security.
  • Monitor app stability, crash reports, and technical performance.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Flap Bird is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures to delete such information.

5. Managing Your Privacy & Advertising ID

You have full control over personalized advertising on your Android device:

  • Go to Settings > Google > Ads on your Android phone.
  • You can choose to "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID" at any time.
  • You can also play Flap Bird completely offline with airplane mode enabled if you prefer not to load network ads.

6. Data Security

We value your trust in playing Flap Bird. All network communications with Google AdMob use industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
